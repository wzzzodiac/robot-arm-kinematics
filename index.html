MODEL NOTE

What changed in v2?

The arm can now switch between two and three revolute joints. The 2-DOF solver uses the classic analytic elbow-up / elbow-down solutions. In 3-DOF mode, the requested TCP orientation defines a wrist point; the first two joints solve that point analytically and the third joint completes the requested orientation. Every solution is checked against joint limits before the robot moves.
