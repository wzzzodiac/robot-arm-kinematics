MODEL NOTE

What is actually happening?

Forward kinematics computes the end-effector position from θ1 and θ2. Inverse kinematics does the opposite: given an XY target, the solver calculates a valid joint configuration for the two-link arm. Targets outside the reachable workspace are rejected instead of negotiating with mathematics.
